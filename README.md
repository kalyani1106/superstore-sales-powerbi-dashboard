# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Superstore
sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product
performance, and regional trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Compare profitability across regions
- Analyze customer segments
- Identify top-performing products
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Provide business insights and recommendations

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Sales: 2.30M
- Total Profit: 286.40K
- Total Quantity: 38K
- Total Discount: 1.56K

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Sales Trend by Month
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Region and Category Filters
- Business Insights & Recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology is the leading sales category.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment.
- West has the highest regional profitability.
- Sales show stronger performance toward the end of the year.
